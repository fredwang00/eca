## Flags & Risks

**Data quality flags:**
- Total equity is growing consistently ($5.0B in Q4 2024 to $9.8B in Q2 2026)—no equity decline flag.
- Operating income has accelerated sharply ($11M in Q4 2024 to $912M in Q2 2026), but the Q4 2024 figure ($11M) includes a $131M one-time SAR acceleration disclosed by Glazer. Underlying Q4 2024 operating income was materially different from the headline; the structured metric captures the distorted figure.
- Adjusted FCF and reported FCF diverge by $33M in Q1 2026 (management: $925M; verified: $891.8M) and $18M in Q2 2026 (management: $1.22B; verified: $1.2016B). Both within expected non-GAAP adjustment ranges but should be tracked for drift.
- Stock-based compensation was $172M/quarter in Q3 2025 (14.6% of revenue), creating a 18-percentage-point GAAP/adjusted operating margin gap. The call does not narrate this gap on the verbal record. This is the most significant recurring financial disclosure gap across the history reviewed.

**Communication-level risks:**
- **Structural CEO/CFO credibility bifurcation.** Every quarter evaluated identifies the same dynamic: Glazer provides accountable, precise disclosure; Karp provides performative rhetoric. This is now a structural pattern (11+ quarters), not a quarterly artifact. If results soften, there is no established CEO communication register for honest setback explanation.
- **Contempt-for-skepticism trap.** Karp's repeated public mockery of analysts creates an accountability vacuum: when the CEO frames all skepticism as intellectual failure, the social cost of bringing bad news disappears internally and externally. This historically precedes periods where problems surface suddenly.
- **International commercial decline is systematically underdisclosed.** International commercial grew 26% in Q2 2026 against 149% U.S. commercial. No strategic explanation, corrective plan, or milestone has been offered across any quarter reviewed. The gap is widening, not closing.
- **TCV quality opacity.** TCV bookings have grown dramatically ($4.3B in Q4 2025, $13.1B remaining deal value in Q2 2026) without disclosure of weighted-average duration, government termination-for-convenience exposure, or renewal-versus-new mix. The metric receives headline treatment without the transparency needed to assess quality.
- **Perishable goals pattern.** Multiple strategic initiatives introduced with enthusiasm and never revisited: Warp Speed (introduced Q2 2024, never quantified), hyperscaler discussions (Q1 2024, never closed), AIPCon product commitments, American Tech Fellowship launch date. The absence of any systematic prior-commitment revisitation process is itself a structural accountability gap.
