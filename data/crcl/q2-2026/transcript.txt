Earnings Call Sentiment
Positive
The call emphasized strong operational and network momentum—robust USDC circulation growth, dramatic on-chain and mint/redeem activity, significant product milestones (OCC charter, Arc Mainnet launch date, DTCC and BlackRock partnerships), upgraded other-revenue guidance driven by Arc, and rapid CPN adoption. Headwinds were present but manageable: lower reserve yields (3.48%, down 66 bps YoY), weak broader digital-asset markets (~40% market-cap decline YoY) that pressured some revenue lines, higher operating spending (adjusted Opex +23% YoY) and reliance on Arc presale milestone recognition for a material portion of near-term guidance. On balance, highlights materially outweigh the lowlights given scalable network effects, strategic partnerships, margin expansion and strong adjusted EBITDA, though execution and macro/regulatory timing risks remain.

Company Guidance
The company raised full‑year other revenue guidance to $310–$330 million (up from $150–$170 million), driven largely by Arc where a $242 million token presale and a pre‑Mainnet $3 billion asset were created and ~75% of Arc milestones (≈$180 million) are expected to be recognized in 2026; the balance of other products is forecast to contribute roughly $130–$150 million. Management also increased RLDC margin guidance to 41.7%–43.7% (from 38%–40%) — noting that excluding Arc revenue the margin should be near the midpoint of the prior range — and left adjusted operating expense guidance unchanged at $570–$585 million while saying they expect to land toward the higher end. Longer term, Circle reiterated a multiyear through‑cycle USDC growth target of ~40% CAGR and cited third‑party stablecoin market scenarios implying 27%–77% CAGRs to 2030.

Lowlights
USDC Circulation Growth and Scale
USDC circulation ended Q2 at $73.3 billion, up ~19-20% year-over-year, with average circulation reaching an all-time high of $76.5 billion during the quarter.

Daily on-chain transaction volume averaged $163 billion in Q2, up 151% year-over-year; total transactional volume with USDC grew ~151% YoY to nearly $15 trillion, and average minting/redemption was $1.9 billion per day (up 105% YoY).

Revenue, Profitability and Margin Expansion
Total revenue and reserve income was $701 million in Q2 (up 7% YoY). Revenue less distribution cost margin was 41.2% (up ~3 percentage points YoY). Adjusted EBITDA grew 8% YoY to $143 million with a 50% adjusted EBITDA margin.

Platform and Product Milestones (Arc, Bank Charters)
Received final OCC National Trust Bank charter and a New York limited purpose trust charter; announced Arc Mainnet launch on September 16 with >100 partners on private mainnet and testnet processing >0.5 billion transactions across nearly 3 million wallets.

Major Strategic Partnerships for Arc
Announced initial Arc validator cohort including top global asset manager, leading clearing firm, major exchanges and payments networks; strategic partnerships with DTCC (tokenized DTC-custodied assets on Arc) and BlackRock (BUIDL deployment with native USDC integration).

Arc Token Presale and Other Revenue Upgrade
Executed a $242 million Arc token presale in Q2 and created a $3 billion asset pre-Mainnet; raised full-year 'other revenue' guidance to $310M–$330M (from $150M–$170M) and included ~$180M of expected 2026 presale milestone revenue.

Payments Network (CPN) Traction
CPN reached nearly $15 billion annualized TPV on a trailing 30-day basis at end of Q2 and grew to $23 billion as of July 31 (130% growth since last report); enrolled 175 financial institutions (≈30% quarter-over-quarter growth) and now reaches >58 countries.

Expansion in Other Digital Assets and Market Positions
EURC grew 2.2x YoY to remain the largest digital euro; USYC grew 10x YoY to over $3 billion, becoming the largest tokenized money market fund; USDC reached nearly 70% share of stablecoin transaction volume in June (up from 36% in Q2 last year).
