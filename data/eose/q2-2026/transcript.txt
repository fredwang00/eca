Earnings Call Sentiment
Positive
The call balanced material near-term challenges (large GAAP losses driven by mark-to-market volatility, deeply negative adjusted gross margins, and a deliberate near-term production trade-off to consolidate manufacturing) with strong operational progress and a clear path to margin improvement. Management reported record revenue, record backlog and cube shipments, large YoY and sequential production and cost improvements (material and labor), a healthy cash balance ($364M), a sizable $24.6B pipeline (112 GWh) with differentiated multi-hour exposure, and a detailed plan (Thorn Hill consolidation + Frontier Power USA) to convert pipeline into operating assets and improve margins. Given the magnitude of operational gains, production scale, pipeline growth and a concrete cost-out roadmap that management expects to drive large margin improvement over the next 12 months, the positive execution momentum and strategic initiatives outweigh the current near-term financial losses and risks.

Company Guidance
The company tightened 2026 revenue guidance to $300–$350 million (low end ~2.5x 2025 revenue and >19x 2024), with H2 > H1, Q4 > Q3 and the low end implying roughly $50 million of second‑half growth over the $126 million delivered in H1; Q2 revenue was $68.8 million (up 351% YoY, 21% sequentially) on record backlog (~$807 million) and record shipments, ending the quarter with $364 million cash; operationally Turtle Creek reached an annualized June throughput of ~1.5 GWh, H1 cube output was 17% above all of 2025 (matching 2025 volume in 164 days) with scrap dollars down 63%, Line 2 cycle times ~10% faster (bipolar 11% faster) while Line 2 contributed ~1% of Q2 production as Thorn Hill ramps toward 24/7 to hit the upside of guidance; fleet metrics include 6.5 GWh cumulative discharge (up ~0.5 GWh since last call), >3.9 million cycles (Z3 >1.1 million), fleet average round‑trip efficiency 78% (20–100–20 SoC) with top‑end tests >90% and field durations 2.5–14 hours; financials reflect a Q2 adjusted gross loss of $42.9 million (adjusted gross margin -62%), gross loss $48.8 million, net loss $276 million and adjusted EBITDA loss $71.4 million (-104% margin), and management outlined a plan to drive >72 percentage points of adjusted gross margin improvement over 12 months (targeting ~25 p.p. material cost reduction, ~20 p.p. conversion cost, ~20 p.p. project/field service and ~8 p.p. yield gains) plus an expected 10–15% conversion‑cost uplift from consolidating lines (≈9‑month payback) as the path to profitability and positive adjusted gross margin.


Highlights

Lowlights
Record Revenue and Strong Top-Line Momentum
Q2 revenue of $68.8M, up 351% year-over-year and 21% sequentially. First half revenue just under $126M, which already exceeds total 2025 revenue. Management tightened 2026 revenue outlook to $300M–$350M and described a clear H2>H1 shape with Q4>Q3.

Record Backlog, Large Pipeline and Multi-hour Market Positioning
Record backlog of ~$807M with a pipeline of $24.6B (~112 GWh), up 31% year-over-year. 51% of the pipeline is >8 hours duration and 32% is data-center related — positioning the company in higher-duration, differentiated market segments.

Fleet Performance and Field References
Fleet has cumulatively discharged 6.5 GWh and run >3.9 million cycles; Z3 fleet >1.1 million cycles. Average round-trip efficiency across 20–100–20 SoC is 78% (fleet average under real duty cycles) and top-end performance has exceeded 90% in entitlement cases.

Manufacturing Scale and Productivity Gains
Cube shipments reached record levels: cube deliveries up 207% year-over-year and 20% sequentially. Turtle Creek cube output rose 20% sequentially and achieved an annualized ~1.5 GWh production rate in June. H1 2026 produced 17% more cubes than all of 2025 and matched 2025 volume in 164 days. Scrap dollars down 63% on the same volume.

Cost Reductions in Materials and Labor
Material costs improved 10% sequentially (and have fallen ~12.5% in <1 year since DawnOS launch); excluding timing items material cost per cube improved 1% sequentially. Direct labor cost per cube declined 20% sequentially. Manufacturing overhead per cube improved 4% sequentially (≈16% improvement at Turtle Creek standalone).

Thorn Hill Automation Upside
Thorn Hill Line 2 shows initial performance gains: battery cycle times ~10% faster and bipolar cycle times ~11% faster than Line 1. Management expects consolidating Line 1 into Thorn Hill could deliver an incremental 10%–15% reduction in conversion costs with an estimated ~9-month payback.

Frontier Power USA Capital & Execution Vehicle
Frontier Power USA raised $263M gross proceeds to support an estimated $1B project deployment funnel. The JV pipeline: 16 GWh opportunity, 5 GWh selected/under active due diligence, 1.8 GWh under construction. First projects expected online by Q3 2027; a pre-existing financed project accounted for ~80% of Q2 revenue and is expected to deliver mid-teen returns.

Cash Position and Cash Flow Discipline
Ended Q2 with $364M cash. Operational cash use closely tracked adjusted EBITDA loss during the quarter (near 100% free cash flow conversion from operations in the quarter). Working capital did not consume incremental cash despite 21% sequential revenue growth.

Commercial Traction and International Agreements
Signed a 750 MWh master supply agreement with CAPAC (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), awarded a strategic partnership under the U.S. Golden Dome program, and announced a $100M purchase order for Phase 1 of the Blanquilla project in ERCOT. Six customers placed orders in the quarter (4 new, 2 repeat).

Clear Margin Improvement Roadmap
Management outlined four drivers that together target >72 percentage points of adjusted gross margin improvement over the next 12 months if executed: ~25 points from material cost reductions, ~20 points from conversion cost improvements (consolidation into Thorn Hill), ~20 points from project/field service productivity, and ~8 points from yield improvements.
