Earnings Call Sentiment
Positive
The call presents strong commercial traction and market validation: $4.0 billion of ARR contracted, validating demand; accelerating pricing (3-year +125% since November), major GPU financings ($6.5B in recent months) and delivery of Horizon 1 with NVIDIA validation are material positives. The company is in an aggressive scaling phase with large near‑term CapEx ($25B–$30B FY27), timing risks for revenue recognition as capacity comes online late in the December quarter, and substantial noncash impairments and a heavy net loss tied to the transition away from mining. Overall the positives — sizable contracted ARR, robust financing capability, pricing momentum, technical validation and a deep pipeline — materially outweigh the transitional lowlights tied to decommissioning and high near-term spend.

Company Guidance
The company guided to heavy near‑term growth and funding: FY‑27 CapEx of approximately $25–$30 billion (covering 2026 deployments, remaining air‑cooled 2027 builds and a large portion of 2H‑2027 liquid‑cooled work), roughly 300 MW of IT load delivered in 2026 and another 0.5 GW in 2027 (taking gross platform capacity to ~1.2 GW in 2027), more than $4 billion of ARR already contracted (with $1 billion operating today and >$4 billion expected by the end of the December quarter), an announced portfolio of >5 GW and a multi‑GW pipeline, FY‑26 headcount nearly tripled with >4,000 people mobilized, GPU financing momentum (≈$6.5 billion raised in the past 3 months; $3.6 billion at ~6% for Microsoft; $2.8 billion equipment financing including $2.4 billion at 9%), ~ $19 billion secured funding over 12 months (~$16 billion from prepayments/GPU financing/convertibles and ~$3 billion equity), ~ $14 billion of existing cash/committed GPU financing/prepayments (including $7.6 billion cash at 30 June, $1.7 billion restricted), targeting an additional ~$8 billion of GPU financing/prepayments, customer prepayments funding ~45–55% of GPU CapEx (in some packages exceeding 100% of GPU CapEx with financing), expected data center and GPU CapEx to rise ~15–20% with revenue increases expected to outpace that, and pricing trends showing 3‑year contract pricing up ~125% since November and 5‑year up ~70% (recent 3‑year deals >$20M per IT MW with active discussions around ~$25M per IT MW and ~2‑year compute payback); mining operations to be effectively decommissioned by end‑December 2026.

Rapid Increase in Demand Signals

Strong Pricing Momentum

Significant GPU Financing and Funding Raised
Over the past 3 months IREN closed ~$6.5 billion of GPU financing, including $3.6 billion of investment-grade GPU financing for the Microsoft contract at ~6% weighted average and $2.8 billion of non-investment-grade equipment financing (including $2.4 billion at 9% fixed). Over the past 12 months the company has secured ~ $19 billion in funding (~$16 billion customer prepayments, GPU financing and convertible notes plus ~$3 billion equity).

Prepayments Funding Meaningful Share of GPU CapEx
Recent customer prepayments are funding ~45%–55% of GPU CapEx on recent deals; combined with high GPU financing leverage (e.g., 90% gearing on some transactions) the prepayment+financing mix often covers or exceeds associated GPU CapEx.

Horizon 1 Delivery and Technical Validation

Aggressive Build and Pipeline

Organizational and Software Progress

Balance Sheet and Liquidity Position
