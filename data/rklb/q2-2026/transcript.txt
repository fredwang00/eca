Earnings Call Sentiment
Positive
Overall the call conveyed strong commercial momentum and operational execution: record revenue, sizable bookings and backlog, margin outperformance in Q2, robust liquidity and meaningful progress on Neutron development and strategic M&A (Mynaric, Motiv, pending Iridium). Offsetting risks include elevated cash burn and negative free cash flow driven by Neutron tail production and R&D, a sequential decline in Launch Services revenue due to accounting mix, margin pressure from recent acquisitions and schedule risk for Neutron's first flight. On balance, the positive commercial wins, backlog, margin beats and cash position outweigh the near-term cash and schedule pressures.

Company Guidance
For Q3 the company guided revenue of $250–$265 million (midpoint ≈ +10% QoQ), GAAP gross margin of 29–31% and non‑GAAP gross margin of 35–37%, GAAP operating expenses of $143–$149 million (non‑GAAP $121–$127M), net interest income of ~$21 million, an adjusted EBITDA loss of $17–$23 million, basic weighted‑average shares of ~641 million, and continued elevated negative non‑GAAP free cash flow (excluding financing). Management also said ~45.5% of the current ~$2.36 billion backlog is expected to convert to revenue within 12 months, capex will remain elevated as Neutron development continues (Q2 capex was $26M), and liquidity was roughly $2.4 billion after $1.08 billion raised via the ATM.
