## Candor Trajectory

**Grade progression:** C+ (Q1 2023, 2.4) → C+ (Q3 2023, 2.4) → C+ (Q4 2023, 2.3) → C (Q1 2024, 2.0) → B (Q2 2024, 2.55) → C+ (Q3 2024, 2.55) → C+ (Q4 2024, 2.55) → C+ (Q1 2025, 2.55) → C+ (Q2 2025, 2.4) → C+ (Q3 2025, 2.55) → B (Q4 2025, 2.8) → B (Q1 2026, 3.075) → B (Q2 2026, 2.8)

**Direction:** Improving, with two identifiable inflection points.

**First inflection — Q1 2024 (trough):** The grade dropped to a flat C (2.0), the series low. The call disclosed a price investment program touching "thousands of items" with zero quantification of cost, scope, or vendor funding. Four executives repeated the Q2 comp recovery thesis without attaching a number, category, or mechanism. The relay-answer architecture in Q&A allowed four of McShane's closing questions to go unanswered. This represents the weakest disclosure posture in the dataset.

**Second inflection — Q2 2024 (recovery):** The first B in the series (2.55). Fiddelke's financial section provided gross margin (28.9%, +190 bps), ROIC (16.6%), shrink decomposition by quarter (Q2/Q3/Q4 sequencing with basis-point estimates), and sortation center unit economics (20% lower delivery cost, 60,000 packages/day capacity by 2028). This specificity set a new floor for financial disclosure.

**Third inflection — Q4 2025 / Q1 2026:** The composite crossed B cleanly (2.8 and 3.075). The new CFO Jim Lee established a template of proactive disaggregation; Fiddelke voluntarily disclosed that Q1 2026's two-year stack was "well below the level we aspire to" during a beat quarter—a behavior pattern not present in prior CEO communication. Q2 2026 maintained B at 2.8, though the revenue figure discrepancy flagged below requires resolution before accepting that trajectory as fully validated.

**Structural pattern:** Financial candor (Dimension 1) has consistently scored B across most quarters and is the grade's anchor. Strategic clarity (Dimension 2) has never exceeded C in this dataset except in Q4 2025 and Q1/Q2 2026. FOG Index (Dimension 4) has never exceeded C. These two dimensions are the persistent drag.

## Key Commitments

### Signed
No named-counterparty contracts with disclosed dollar values appear in any transcript. All brand partnerships (Marks & Spencer, Ulta Beauty, Taylor Swift, Wicked/Universal Studios, Champion, Peloton, Tom Holland's Bero, Blogilates, Disney, Marvel, Nintendo, Apple) are described as assortment relationships with no disclosed terms, duration, or financial obligation. No revenue figure attached.

### Aspirational
- **$15 billion incremental revenue over five years** (introduced Q4 2024 FCM): Named multiple times, never decomposed by category, year, or initiative. Surfaced by an analyst in Q2 2025 and not proactively updated by management.
- **~300 new stores by 2035** (Q1 2026): Specific and verifiable. Seven opened in Q1 2026; 23+ required for 2026 commitment to hold.
- **Target Plus GMV to $5 billion by 2030** (Q1 2025): GMV growing >20% in Q1 2025, >40% in Q4 2024, 60% in Q1 2026. No current-year GMV dollar figure disclosed, making the 2030 target untrackable against a base.
- **China sourcing below 25% by end of 2026** (Q1 2025, Gomez): Named as a specific, falsifiable target. Baseline: 30% at time of statement (down from 60% in 2017).
- **Target Circle 360 membership "more than triple" over three years** (Q4 2024): Membership doubled in FY2025 per Q4 2025 FCM. Intermediate pace implied by the three-year goal not disclosed.
- **Roundel mid-teens growth** (recurring): Cited as an ongoing expectation across Q3 2023, Q4 2023, Q1 2024, and Q3 2024. Q2 2026 cited "nearly 20%." No absolute revenue figure disclosed despite Roundel being described as a "$1.5 billion of value" vehicle since Q4 2023 FCM.
- **Operating margin back to "pre-pandemic levels"** (recurring): Cited since Q3 2023. The specific prior target of 6% EBIT was directly retired without acknowledgment in Q4 2024 FCM (Gutman asked; Lee deflected). Current language: "approximately 20 basis points higher" in FY2026 guidance from 4.6% FY2025 base.
- **Tariff offset "vast majority"** (Q1 2025, Gomez): Claimed Target would offset the "vast majority" of incremental tariff exposure. Q2 2026 recorded a $994M tariff refund—context for this offset claim requires the refund to be excluded from the assessment of structural mitigation.
- **Enterprise Acceleration Office** (Q1 2025): Announced without goals, timelines, or metrics. Fiddelke deferred: "I'll have far more details to share over time." Q2 2025 referenced it; no subsequent quantified deliverables surfaced in available transcripts.
- **Beauty Studio in 600+ stores by fall 2026** (Q2 2026): Specific store count and timing. Verifiable at Q3 2026 call.
- **Dry grocery reset: "largest in over a decade"** (Q1 2026): Named as a Q2 2026 execution event. "Nearly half of center store grocery assortment" being transitioned. Q2 2026 confirms execution underway; sales lift from new 3,000 items growing "more than 50% over prior assortment."

## FOG Patterns

**Recurring phrases across quarters (with quarters of appearance):**

- **"Laser-focused":** Q1 2023 ("laser-focused on improving traffic"), Q2 2024, Q3 2025, Q4 2025 FCM (Lee: "laser-focused on making sure we get the right value"), Q1 2026. This phrase appears in six of thirteen quarters without any measurable qualifier. No improvement in precision over time.
- **"Being Target" / "make Target, Target":** Q3 2024 (multiple Q&A instances, including direct response to competitive strategy question), Q3 2025, Q4 2025 FCM. Circular self-reference deployed as strategic content.
- **"Resilient" (consumer):** Q1 2023 through Q2 2024 consistently. Upgraded to "resourceful" in Q3 2024 and credited as an observation upgrade. Reverted to "resilient" language in Q4 2025 and Q1 2026.
- **"Green shoots":** Q3 2024 Q&A, at least four instances. Asserts optimism without specifying which shoots, at what growth rate, or over what timeline.
- **"More to come":** Q3 2023 (CapEx composition and 2024 operating margin drivers both deferred with this phrase). Q1 2025 (Acceleration Office: "I'll have far more details to share over time"). Q3 2025 (multiple deferreds to March FCM). A structural deferral device rather than a timing disclosure.
- **"Play the long game":** Q3 2024 Q&A, deployed as a deflection to analyst pressure about whether the current approach should change.
- **"Tarzhay" / "Tarzhay magic":** Q4 2024 FCM through Q1 2025. Brand mythology substituting for strategic specificity. Present in Q2 2025 and Q3 2025 prepared remarks. Partially displaced by "style-led, design-led" language in Q4 2025 and later.
- **"We couldn't be more excited/confident":** Q4 2025 (multiple). Classic unsupported superlative at moments of highest visibility.
- **"Delight" as operational concept:** Q4 2025 and Q2 2026. Fiddelke installs "delight" as "a critical filter for decisions" without defining a metric, mechanism, or benchmark for when delight is or is not being delivered.

**Trajectory:** FOG has not improved materially. The FOG Index dimension has scored C in every quarter evaluated. The specific phrases rotate—"Tarzhay magic" recedes, "style-led" enters—but the cliché density in prepared remarks remains consistently above the level that earns a B. Q&A performance is meaningfully better than prepared remarks in every quarter where this distinction can be assessed; the FOG is a scripted-content problem more than a conversational one.

## Flags & Risks

**Structural accountability gaps:**
- **The 6% EBIT target was retired without acknowledgment.** Introduced at the February 2023 Investor Day, deflected in Q1 2023 ("nothing new to share"), quietly absent from the March 2024 FCM, and directly questioned by Gutman at the March 2025 FCM. Lee's response pivoted to general margin tailwinds without confirming, denying, or explaining the target's status. The phrase "pre-pandemic levels" has substituted for a specific margin target across three consecutive FCMs.
- **The waterfall refusal is a recurring accountability shield.** When asked in Q4 2023 FCM to decompose comp growth drivers, management explicitly declined ("we're not going to provide you the waterfall today"). This pattern recurs: the $15 billion revenue target has no category bridge, year-by-year phasing, or initiative attribution. Targets that cannot be disaggregated cannot be falsified.
- **Guidance raised then lowered cycle (Q3 2024).** Target raised Q3 2024 guidance, then guided the full year to the bottom of the original March range. No explicit reconciliation of what management believed at the time of the raise versus actual results. The Q3 2024 call acknowledges the miss in sentiment ("disappointing") but not analytically.
- **Unquantified cost surprises (Q3 2024).** Two separate analysts asked for the basis-point magnitude of "unique" Q3 costs (general liability, health care, supply chain repositioning). Both received category descriptions without dollar amounts or basis points. Lee offered "approximately 1% of the increase in SG&A" for liability and health care—a ratio without a denominator anchor.
- **Revenue figure discrepancy (Q1 2026, Q2 2026).** Management-cited net sales diverge from structured ground truth by material amounts. Q2 2026 operating margin cited at 9.6% is inconsistent with $1.135B GAAP operating income on $25.443B revenue (~4.5%). Requires 10-Q reconciliation before accepting narrative figures.
- **Free cash flow absent from narrative disclosure.** FCF appears in structured data (Q3 2025: $1,009M; Q4 2025: $149M; Q1 2026: $2,192M; Q2 2026: -$319M) but is not mentioned in any prepared remarks. Q2 2026's negative FCF (-$319M) against $5B annual CapEx guidance was not addressed. For a company leading with capital allocation priorities, this is a consistent omission.
- **Equity trend:** Total equity is stable to modestly growing ($15,420M → $15,501M → $16,165M → $16,395M across the four quarters with structured data), but the Q4 2025 free cash flow of $149M suggests the company is consuming nearly all generated cash in that quarter. No deterioration signal yet but warrants monitoring given the $5B CapEx commitment.
- **Target Plus GMV base undisclosed.** The 2030 target of $5B GMV is stated without a disclosed current-year figure. Growth rates (>20%, >35%, 40%, 60% sequentially) are provided without an anchor, making the 2030 target untrackable.

**Communication-level risks:**
- The "perishable goal" pattern is active. Goals introduced and quietly retired without acknowledgment include: the 6% EBIT margin target, the $6% EPS aspiration (Q1 2023), the initial shrink recovery timeline, and the $2–3B efficiency savings road map (introduced Q4 2023, not subsequently quantified on an annual basis).
- The Enterprise Acceleration Office was announced in Q1 2025 as a structural organizational change with no goals or timeline. No subsequent quantified deliverables appear in available transcripts.
- The Q2 2026 tariff refund ($994M) is a one-time item that elevates reported margins materially. Lee proactively disaggregates it, but future quarters will lap this benefit. If the normalization of margins in Q3 2026 is not clearly communicated, guidance credibility will be tested.
