Earnings Call Sentiment
Positive
The call presented a clear improvement in top-line trends, category momentum (notably Food, Fun101, Beauty, and toys), strong digital and loyalty growth, improved fulfillment metrics, and raised guidance — all supported by strategic investments in stores, remodels, supply chain and AI. However, a meaningful portion of margin and EPS upside in the quarter was driven by a large one-time tariff refund ($994M pretax / $1.65 EPS), apparel and home remain underperforming and SG&A is rising. Management emphasized that underlying performance excluding refunds is healthy (adjusted EPS ex-refunds up ~20% in Q2) and that they are early in a multi-year transformation. Overall, the positives and momentum reported materially outweigh the caveats and one-time benefits, though sustainable improvement will depend on continued execution across apparel, home, inventory consistency and disciplined capital deployment.

Company Guidance
Target raised its full‑year outlook, now expecting net sales to grow around 5% (up 1 percentage point from the prior outlook) and full‑year operating margin (excluding IEEPA tariff refunds) to be roughly 0.5 percentage points higher than last year’s adjusted 4.6% rate; EPS guidance was increased from $7.50–$8.50 to $9.90–$10.90 (the updated range includes a $1.65 benefit from Q2 tariff refunds, which were a ~$994 million pretax reduction of cost of sales, and excluding those refunds the midpoint is about $0.75 higher than the prior midpoint), management reiterated it expects ~ $5.0 billion of CapEx for the year ($2.4 billion deployed YTD, up ~30%), paid $518 million of dividends in Q2 (just over $1.0 billion YTD) while targeting a longer‑term ~40% payout ratio, and said it expects capacity to resume share repurchases in H2 subject to operating results, cash generation and maintaining its middle‑A credit ratings.


Highlights

Lowlights
Revenue and Comparable Sales Growth
Net sales of $26.5 billion, up 5.3% year-over-year; comparable sales +3.8% (store comps +2.7%, digital comps +8.7%); traffic growth +3.6% with average ticket roughly flat.

Improved Profitability and EPS
GAAP and adjusted EPS of $4.11 vs $2.05 a year ago. Excluding tariff refunds, EPS was approximately 20% higher than prior year. Operating margin improved to 9.6% from 5.2% last year; excluding tariff refund impact, operating margin was ~70 basis points higher YoY.

Raised Full-Year Guidance
Full-year net sales growth guidance raised to around 5% (1 percentage point above prior outlook). EPS guidance raised to $9.90–$10.90 (this range includes $1.65 of tariff refunds recognized in Q2); excluding tariff refunds the midpoint of guidance is $0.75 higher than prior midpoint.

Category and Merchandising Momentum
Notable category strength: snack sales running >15% ahead of last year; LEGO sales +30%; plush sales +20%; Heyday $10 headphones sales +35%. Q2 transitions (largest volume of in-store transitions in a decade) boosted culture-driven categories and limited-time collaborations (e.g., LoveShackFancy and Pokemon).

Digital & Loyalty Growth
Digital strength: same-day delivery growth >25%; digital comparable sales +8.7%. Marketplace and media growth: Target+ marketplace GMV grew >40%, Roundel gross billings grew nearly 20%, and Target Circle 360 membership revenue increased >40%.

Operational Improvements & Fulfillment
Inventory reliability on top/frequency items reached multiyear highs; overall inventory $13.2 billion, up ~3% YoY. Same-day and next-day fulfillment units were nearly 30% higher than last year. Trailing 12‑month after-tax ROIC improved to 15.4% from 14.3%.

Strategic Investments and Capacity Expansion
Year-to-date capital expenditures ~ $2.4 billion (up nearly 30% YoY); full-year CapEx expected about $5 billion. Opened 17 new stores in the quarter (24 full-size stores YTD) and advanced >100 remodels toward ~130 remodels planned for the year.

Technology & AI Initiatives
Accelerating tech investments: partnership activity with OpenAI and Google Gemini; digital traffic from external AI platforms growing more than 3.5x the industry year-over-year; new Chief AI Officer hired and rollout of tools like Proxima (digital twin for inventory flow testing).
