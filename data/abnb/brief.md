# Airbnb, Inc. (ABNB) — Earnings Call Brief

**Coverage:** Q1 2023 through Q4 2025 (10 quarters)

---

## Candor Trajectory

**Grade history:** B+ (3.15) → B (3.25) → B (3.00) → B (2.55) → B (3.25) → B (3.00) → B- (2.695) → B (2.55) → B (2.55) → B (3.25) → B (3.25) → B (3.25)

**Direction: Stagnating with a trough.** The series opened at its highest point (Q1 2023, 3.15) and has oscillated within a narrow B band for ten consecutive quarters, never returning to B+ territory and never breaking below C+. The notable inflection is **Q4 2023** (2.55), driven by the "expanding beyond the core" narrative collapsing into sustained FOG—Dimensions 2 and 4 each graded C, the only instance in the series. The score partially recovered in **Q1 2024** (3.25) on Mertz's debut as CFO, whose consistently precise Q&A has been the primary floor under each subsequent grade. A second trough appeared in **Q3 2024** (2.695) as FOG in Chesky's prepared remarks and a material closing-statement error on free cash flow figures both weighed. **Q4 2025** closes the series at 3.25—the ceiling repeatedly reached but never exceeded. The communication quality has bifurcated structurally: Mertz earns A-level marks across nearly every quarter; Chesky's contributions oscillate between B and C depending on how much of the call is strategic vision versus operational reporting. The composite grade reflects this average, not either speaker individually.

## Key Commitments

### Signed
- **GamePlanner.AI acquisition** (Q4 2023): Capital deployed for acquisition described as led by "the Co-Founder and original developer of Siri." No disclosed consideration, no disclosed synergy metrics.
- **Cross-currency fee rollout** (Q4 2023, executed April 2024): Scope disclosed as ~20% of GBV; fee magnitude ~1% for most transactions. Q1 2024 confirmed testing began as stated.
- **Co-hosting marketplace launch** (Q3 2024, executed October 2024): Named product, 10 countries, 10,000 co-hosts at launch, 20,000 applicants in three weeks. By Q4 2025: 100,000 listings managed by co-hosts, expanding to Japan and Korea.
- **Reserve Now, Pay Later** (disclosed Q4 2025): U.S. launch with 70% take-up among eligible customers (Q3 2025); cancellation rate disclosed as moving from ~16% to ~17% baseline; expanded to cross-border U.S. stays. Three specific product features (RNPL, updated cancellation policy, simplified fee structure) attributed to "200 basis points of NIKEs growth and 300 basis points of GBV growth in Q4 2025."
- **May 13, 2025 product launch**: Chesky committed to this date across Q4 2024 and Q1 2025 calls. Confirmed as "2025 Summer Release" in Q3 2025 call. Experiences described as relaunched on that date.
- **Share repurchase programs**: $2.5B authorization (Q1 2023); $6B authorization (Q4 2023); new $6B program (Q2 2025). Cumulative: ~9% reduction in fully diluted share count since 2022.

### Aspirational
- **"1 to 2 new businesses generating $1 billion or more of revenue incrementally per year"** (first stated Q3 2024): No named businesses, no baseline year, no definition of "incremental." Referenced by analysts in Q3 2025; Chesky pivoted to enthusiasm without confirming or retracting the commitment.
- **Hotels as "meaningfully larger percent of the overall business" by end of 2026** (Q4 2025): Three pilot markets named (L.A., NYC, Madrid); "single-digit percent" of nights booked baseline stated; no quantified exit target.
- **Advertising as "easily a billion-dollar opportunity"** (Q4 2024): Explicitly deprioritized for 2025. No timeline provided.
- **Loyalty/membership program**: Described as "in testing" or "not imminently" across at least Q4 2024, Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q3 2025, and Q4 2025. No launch criteria, no timeline, no defined test parameters disclosed in any quarter.
- **AI search**: Described as "imminently" launching in Q3 2025; Q4 2025 states "live in testing for a small percent of traffic." No user adoption metric disclosed.
- **Experiences materiality**: Chesky stated "it's going to take 3 to 5 years" (Q3 2025). No supply targets, GMV targets, or booking volume benchmarks disclosed in any quarter since Q1 2023.
- **International reacceleration**: "Low teens" growth aspiration referenced (Q3 2025) as attributed to a September conference. Not ratified as formal guidance on any call.

## FOG Patterns

**The FOG is structural, not incidental.** Across ten quarters, the same linguistic patterns recur in Chesky's prepared remarks and vision-section Q&A, while Mertz consistently avoids them. The patterns have not improved over time; they have migrated from topic to topic as new strategic initiatives replace older ones.

**Recurring specific phrases (cited across multiple quarters):**
- "Expand beyond our core" / "expanding beyond the core" — named strategic pillar that has produced zero falsifiable milestones since Q1 2023.
- "We're just getting started" — Q2 2023 closing; functionally identical language in Q4 2024 ("2025 marks the start of Airbnb's next chapter").
- "I'm really excited" / "We're incredibly excited" / "I'm incredibly proud" — appears in closing remarks of every evaluated quarter without specific attribution.
- "The biggest ideas Airbnb has are in front of us" — Q1 2023; "the opportunity ahead is even bigger than what you're seeing today" — Q4 2025. Same content, different words.
- "Metronomic" — used twice in Q3 2025 as a substitute for precise product cadence description.
- Amazon analogy (books → categories → AWS) — deployed in Q4 2023, Q1 2024, Q2 2024, Q3 2024, Q4 2024, Q4 2025. The repetition has migrated from illuminating to reflexive.

**Precision has not improved.** The FOG concentration has simply moved: Q1–Q2 2023, FOG was in the "expand beyond core" section. By Q3–Q4 2024, it spread into AI commentary. By Q4 2025, it persists in hotels, loyalty, and the full strategic vision section. The Chesky/Mertz quality gap is itself a stable pattern—it has been noted explicitly in analysts for Q2 2024, Q3 2024, Q4 2024, Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q3 2025, and Q4 2025.

## Flags & Risks

**Data quality flags:**
- **Free cash flow discrepancy (Q4 2023):** Chesky cited "full year free cash flow of $3.8 billion" in prepared remarks but "trailing 12-month free cash flow of $2.8 billion" in closing remarks—a $1 billion gap never reconciled on the call.
- **CEO metric error (Q3 2024):** Chesky's closing summary stated "trailing 12 months cash flow is $1.1 billion" at a "38% margin"—but $1.1 billion was the quarterly figure; the TTM figure cited earlier in the same call was $4.1 billion.
- **Non-GAAP adjustment unexplained (Q4 2023):** $249M GAAP loss adjusted to $489M net income via "nonrecurring tax items"—a $738M swing with no line-item explanation on the call.

**Communication-level risks:**
- **Chronic deferral pattern on loyalty, advertising, and AI search.** Each has been "in testing," "not the most perishable opportunity," or "coming soon" for three or more consecutive quarters. None has a disclosed launch criterion.
- **"$1 billion per new business" commitment has no accountability structure.** Stated Q3 2024, not ratified or retracted in Q3 or Q4 2025. No named businesses, no timeline, no baseline.
- **North America deceleration underdiagnosed.** NA grew "low single digits" in Q2 2025 and "mid-singles" in Q4 2025. Neither quarter produced a frank proactive analysis of what is causing the growth gap in the company's largest market.
- **New business milestones absent.** $200M invested across hotels, experiences, and services without disclosed success criteria in any quarter. The phrase "it's early" has appeared in this context since Q1 2023 for Experiences specifically.
- **Metric redefinition risk.** Q2 2025 introduced "nights and seats booked" as the headline volume metric. Mertz acknowledged seats are "immaterial" today. As experiences scale, the accommodation-only growth rate will become less visible under this combined metric.

## Verify Next Quarter

*(Carrying forward Q4 2025 tracking commitments for the Q1 2026 call)*

1. **AI customer service automation rate.** Q4 2025 baseline: ~29–30% of English/North America tickets automated. Chesky stated this will be "significantly more than 30%" and available in "all languages" within approximately one year. Ask for the updated automation rate and whether language expansion is on track.

2. **Hotel supply exit target for 2026.** Mertz committed to "meaningfully larger percent" of overall business by year-end 2026 from a single-digit percent baseline. Demand a percentage or range. If "meaningfully" remains undefined after Q1, flag as negative accountability signal.

3. **Project Hawaii revenue attribution.** Chesky stated this "will deliver hundreds of millions more" in 2026 revenue. Look for feature-level attribution language comparable to Q4's "200 basis points of NIKEs growth" disclosure. If the claim is not revisited with specifics, flag.

4. **Reserve Now Pay Later global expansion.** Mertz specified expansion to "more guests globally and to cross-border stays in the U.S." Verify: (a) whether rollout is named specifically, (b) whether the cancellation curve at scale matches the ~17% test baseline, and (c) whether ADR lift from longer lead times is maintained.

5. **Loyalty program.** This is now in at least its fifth consecutive quarter of "in testing" without a defined launch trigger. If Q1 2026 produces another "testing various benefits" response with no timeline, score Dimension 2 negatively regardless of narrative quality.

6. **"1 to 2 new businesses at $1B+ incremental revenue" — formal status check.** Ask directly: Is this commitment still the target? If management neither confirms nor retracts it, treat as a perishable goal in retirement.
