# Airbnb, Inc. (ABNB) — Earnings Call Brief

---

## Candor Trajectory

**Grade history:** Q1 2023: B+ (3.15) → Q2 2023: B (3.25) → Q3 2023: B (3.00) → Q4 2023: B (2.55) → Q1 2024: B (3.25) → Q2 2024: B (3.00) → Q3 2024: B− (2.695) → Q4 2024: B (2.55) → Q1 2025: B (2.55) → Q2 2025: B (3.25) → Q3 2025: B (3.25) → Q4 2025: B (3.25) → Q1 2026: B+ (3.40) → Q2 2026: B (3.40)

**Direction:** Recovering, with a structural floor around B.

The trajectory divides into three phases. Q1–Q2 2023 established a solid baseline with genuine financial specificity and Chesky's above-average strategic voice. Q4 2023 and Q3 2024 marked the trough — strategic clarity and FOG grades both dropped to C-range when the "expanding beyond the core" narrative dominated calls without a single falsifiable milestone. The recovery began in Q2 2025 and firmed through Q1–Q2 2026, driven primarily by Ellie Mertz's CFO discipline (feature-level revenue attribution, explicit comp-dynamics disclosure, proactive headwind quantification) and Chesky's growing willingness to acknowledge competitive risk and defer near-term expectations on nascent businesses. Q1 2026 earned the highest composite since Q1 2023 on the strength of Vision/Leadership reaching A-level, though FOG and strategic deferral patterns prevent a clean break above B.

The single most consistent pattern across all fourteen quarters: Mertz's segments outperform Chesky's in precision and directness. The CEO/CFO quality gap is itself a communication-level risk.

## Key Commitments

**Signed**

- **Share repurchases:** $2.0B repurchased over the nine months preceding Q1 2023; new $2.5B authorization announced May 2023. By Q2 2026, cumulative buybacks totaled approximately $3.8B over trailing twelve months, $6B new authorization announced in Q4 2023, and an 8–9% reduction in fully diluted share count since 2022 disclosed as an accountability benchmark.
- **Debt financing:** A $2.5B senior unsecured debt offering was disclosed in Q1 2026 with stated rationale: expand financing access, diversify investor base, optimize cost of capital.
- **Airbnb-friendly apartment program (Greystar):** Named counterparty disclosed in Q1 2023; 175 buildings cited, scaling to 250+.
- **GamePlanner.AI acquisition:** Named as acquired in Q4 2023 call, described as led by the co-founder of Siri. No disclosed valuation or specific capability integration.
- **Delta partnership:** Described as a "rev share program" in Q1 2026; no disclosed financial terms or counterparty commitments on nights volume.
- **Single service fee migration:** Mertz committed in Q2 2026 to completing migration of all hosts to a single service fee by year-end. Progress trackable against this date.

**Aspirational**

- **"1 to 2 new businesses generating $1B+ revenue incrementally per year":** Stated Q3 2024; no counterparty, no baseline definition of "incremental," no timeline, no success criteria. Not formally revisited with accountability in any subsequent call.
- **"Expand beyond the core" / new offerings:** Active across Q1 2023–present as a named strategic pillar. Services and experiences described as having "early momentum" with no attach rate targets, revenue milestones, or city-level penetration goals disclosed in any quarter.
- **Hotels as "meaningfully larger percent of overall business" by year-end 2025:** Stated Q4 2024. Not explicitly resolved in Q1–Q2 2026 calls; hotels remain "single-digit % of nights booked" as of Q2 2026.
- **Loyalty/membership program:** Described as "in testing" across at least four consecutive quarters (Q3 2024–Q2 2026) with no disclosed timeline, test criteria, or launch conditions.
- **AI search as transformative interface:** Described since Q2 2023 as generational; rollout to "a very small % of traffic" began August 2026 per Q2 2026 call. No conversion data, milestone, or meaningful traffic share target disclosed.
- **"Low to mid-teens" / "at least mid-teens" revenue growth:** Full-year 2026 guidance raised to mid-teens in Q2 2026; directional rather than a bounded range, covering approximately $800M variance at current run rates.
- **World Cup supply retention:** Chesky predicted the Paris Olympics host retention benchmark (>50% at six months) would replicate for FIFA World Cup cities. Falsifiable when post-tournament data is available.

## FOG Patterns

**Recurring phrases across quarters, never resolved into measurable commitments:**

1. **"Just getting started" / "scratching the surface"** — appears in Q2 2023, Q1 2024, Q2 2024, Q2 2026. Functions as a closing-remarks inflation device; zero informational content.
2. **"There is no silver bullet"** — appears in Q2 2026 prepared remarks and Q&A as a substitute for causal attribution. Ironically, its repeated invocation functions as a silver bullet explanation.
3. **"Incredibly early" / "still small"** — attached to services, experiences, and AI search across Q2 2024–Q2 2026 without ever being paired with a size baseline, growth rate, or milestone for graduation from "early."
4. **"The best [X] in the world" / "world-class"** — attached to AI teams (Q2 2026), design teams (Q3 2025), and infrastructure generally. Never substantiated with comparative evidence.
5. **"Metronomic" improvement cadence** — used twice in Q3 2025 as a substitute for a specific development velocity or release frequency metric.
6. **"We're excited about what we're working on" / "stay tuned"** — appears in Q4 2023, Q1 2025, Q3 2025, Q4 2025 as a temporal deflection mechanism. On four separate occasions, a major strategic disclosure was deferred to a future product launch event (November 2023 Winter Release, May 13 2025, May 20 2026), rendering those earnings calls structurally incomplete on the most material strategic questions.

**Linguistic precision: degraded Q4 2023 through Q1 2025, recovered Q2 2025 onward.** The Q4 2023 AI section was the FOG nadir — a sentence fragment ("So I think this is a zero, zero ballgame") with stacked hedges. The recovery correlates with Mertz taking larger shares of Q&A and with Chesky demonstrating more willingness to offer structured self-critique (Q2 2026: "our B2B strategy is kind of emerging"; Q3 2025: "it's going to take 3 to 5 years").

**Persistent structural gap:** Chesky's prepared remarks consistently carry more FOG than his Q&A. The ratio inverts under analyst pressure — which is the right pattern — but investors who rely on the opening statement rather than Q&A transcript receive a lower-quality information experience.

## Flags & Risks

**Data quality flags:**

- **FCF inconsistency in Q4 2023 call:** Chesky's prepared remarks stated full-year FCF of "$3.8 billion"; his closing remarks cited "$2.8 billion trailing 12-month." The $1B discrepancy was not corrected on the call and required verification against the shareholder letter. Flagged in the Q4 2023 analysis.
- **CEO metric error in Q3 2024 closing:** Chesky cited "$1.1 billion" as the trailing-12-month FCF while $4.1 billion was the correct figure. The $1.1B was the quarterly figure. Disclosed in the Q3 2024 analysis as a preparation or attention-to-precision lapse.
- **Non-GAAP adjustment opacity in Q4 2023:** A $738M swing (from $249M GAAP loss to $489M adjusted income via "nonrecurring tax items") received no line-item explanation on the call. Nature of the items and their genuine non-recurrence were not established.
- **GAAP/non-GAAP gap unexplained in Q1 2026:** GAAP operating income of $86M versus adjusted EBITDA of $519M — a $433M gap attributable primarily to stock-based compensation — was not bridged in prepared remarks.
- **Equity declining:** Total equity dropped from $8,610M (Q3 2025) to $7,636M (Q1 2026), consistent with an aggressive buyback program. Not inherently a risk given strong FCF, but worth monitoring if buyback pace continues relative to earnings accumulation.

**Communication-level risks:**

- **Perpetual deferral pattern on high-value initiatives.** Loyalty programs, sponsored listings, and AI search have each cycled through multiple quarters of "in testing" language without measurable updates. Each call introduces the same vocabulary without resolution. This is the highest-priority accountability gap across the full history.
- **"1 to 2 new billion-dollar businesses per year" commitment (Q3 2024) has no accountability structure** and has not been formally revisited, refined, or retired in any subsequent call. An analyst raised it directly in Q3 2025; Chesky pivoted to pipeline enthusiasm without addressing the specific claim.
- **Strategic opacity via timed announcements.** Four separate major launches have been deferred mid-call to future product events, rendering those earnings calls structurally incomplete on material strategic questions. The pattern normalizes withholding as IR practice.
- **AI cost structure presented as solved.** "Inference costs of Airbnb are de minimis relative to ROI" (Q2 2026) is asserted without disclosing absolute AI spending levels, model dependency concentration, or sensitivity to token price changes. Mertz's guidance update acknowledges "a material increase in AI spend" without quantification — creating an information gap on a line item management has flagged as growing.
- **North America low-single-digit growth unaddressed as underperformance.** Disclosed by Mertz across multiple quarters (Q2 2025, Q3 2025, Q4 2025), but Chesky's prepared remarks consistently pivot past it rather than diagnosing it. The most important market showing the most persistent deceleration receives the least analytical attention.

## Verify Next Quarter

From the Q2 2026 call (tracking forward to Q3 2026, expected November 2026):

1. **AI search rollout:** Chesky committed to beginning testing "this month" (August 2026) at "a very small % of traffic," expanding "over the course of this year." Q3 call must report: (a) current traffic percentage, (b) any conversion or booking data from AI-mediated sessions, and (c) whether the rollout has moved toward default-on status or remains opt-in toggle. No update = negative accountability signal.

2. **Hotel supply progression:** Hotels cited as "single-digit % of nights booked, growing approximately three times faster than homes." Q3 call should show whether the percentage moved, whether the 35% cross-booking rate (hotel-to-home) is holding, and whether supply has expanded beyond the Top 20 cities disclosed in Q2 2026.

3. **Single service fee migration completion:** Mertz committed to completing migration of all hosts by year-end 2026. Q3 call should report approximate completion percentage and any competitive pricing impact on ADR from the migration.

4. **AI voice support launch:** Chesky stated voice support would begin "later this year." Q3 call is the first opportunity to confirm launch and report self-solve metrics comparable to the 40%+ benchmark disclosed for text-based AI customer service.

5. **Full-year guidance floor:** The "at least mid-teens" revenue growth and "at least 35.5%" adjusted EBITDA margin floors should be traceable against Q3 actuals. Any margin compression below the full-year floor requires a specific explanation.

6. **Loyalty program status:** If Q3 2026 produces another "we are testing various benefits" response with no timeline or defined launch criteria, this should be formally graded as a chronic deferral pattern in Dimension 2 (Strategic Clarity) and scored at C or below regardless of other call quality.

7. **World Cup supply retention:** Games run June–July 2026 across 16 host cities. Chesky explicitly set the Paris benchmark (>50% retention at six months). Q3 or Q4 2026 call should report host city listing retention against this stated benchmark — the most direct falsification test of the event-supply flywheel thesis.
