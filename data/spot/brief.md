## Candor Trajectory

**Grade progression:** Q1 2025: B (2.815) → Q2 2025: B (3.015) → Q3 2025: B (2.80) → Q4 2025: B (3.40) → Q1 2026: B (2.55) → Investor Day 2026: B+ (3.42/A-) → Q2 2026: B+ (3.15)

The trajectory is non-linear with two distinct inflection points. The first is **Q4 2025**, where Christian Luiga's financial precision and Daniel Ek's historically-grounded farewell remarks pushed the composite to its pre-Investor Day peak. The second is the **Q1 2026 trough** (2.55), driven by the co-CEO transition: Alex Norstrom's prepared remarks introduced formulaic language ("climb to new mountains previously unimaginable," "takes this to a whole new level") while strategic accountability worsened as the Investor Day deferral entered its second consecutive quarter.

The **Investor Day 2026** composite requires a genre discount — it is a scripted marketing event, not a pressure-tested quarterly disclosure — and its apparent improvement is partially format-driven. The subsequent **Q2 2026** call, returning to the earnings format, confirms a genuine partial recovery (3.15) from Q1's trough, though still below Q4 2025. The persistent weakness is Dimension 2 (Strategic Clarity) and Dimension 4 (FOG Index), both driven by Norstrom's communication register. Luiga has maintained consistent A-/B+ financial precision throughout. The bifurcation between the CFO and co-CEO communication layers is the defining structural pattern.

## FOG Patterns

Recurring vague language has been a consistent weakness across all quarters, concentrated in co-CEO sections and worsening after the leadership transition in January 2026.

**Persistent phrases (multi-quarter):**
- **"Value-to-price ratio"** — Used at least six times in Q1 2025 alone by Norstrom; continued through subsequent quarters as a universal explanation for every positive outcome without quantification. Defined nowhere.
- **"Well positioned"** — Appears in every call, often multiple times, without comparative benchmarking.
- **"Never been in a better position" / "best position we've ever been"** — Variants appear in Q2 2025 and Q3 2025 even in contexts where advertising is underperforming and a leadership departure is being disclosed.
- **"Incredible momentum" / "firing on all cylinders"** — Q3 2025 (Norstrom/Soderstrom prepared remarks); replaced in Q4 2025 and beyond with "solid" and "healthy" — equally content-free.
- **"Early innings"** — Q1 2025 (Ek, multiple instances); functionally unfalsifiable.
- **"When, not if"** — Q3 2025 and thereafter on advertising recovery; converts an accountable prediction into an unchallengeable assertion.

**New FOG introduced post-transition (Q1 2026):**
- "Climb to new mountains previously unimaginable" (Q1 2026)
- "Takes this to a whole new level" (Q1 2026)
- "Much larger next chapter" deferred to Investor Day (Q1 2026)

**Investor Day 2026 FOG** (representative): "The opportunity ahead has never been greater" (identical structure to the Q2 2025 "never been in a better position" pattern); "Every time, our advantage has compounded" (assertion without evidence).

**Direction:** FOG density is improving slightly from the Q1 2026 trough, with Q2 2026 showing more substantive Q&A, particularly from Söderström. However, Norstrom's Q&A deflections (refusing ARPU timing; pivoting to pre-loaded data points on direct questions) represent a qualitative regression from Ek's engagement style. Luiga's financial sections have maintained precision throughout. The linguistic quality of the company's overall disclosure has not improved as a whole since Q4 2025 — it has bifurcated.

## Flags & Risks

**Data quality:**
- Equity dipped Q4 2025 → Q1 2026 ($8,329M → $8,010M); recovered Q2 2026. Attributable to convertible note cash settlement; not a deterioration signal, but worth monitoring.
- CapEx is minimal and volatile ($5M–$23M range); AI compute costs are being run through OpEx, not CapEx. The EUR 200M incremental OpEx is the real capital deployment figure and is not broken down between compute and marketing.
- "Meaningful FCF growth" target has gone unquantified for three consecutive reporting periods (Q4 2025, Q1 2026, Q2 2026). This is a precision regression from Luiga's otherwise exemplary standard.

**Communication-level risks:**
- **Advertising timeline exhaustion.** The H2 2026 ads inflection has been promised across six consecutive communications. Ad-supported revenue grew only 3% YoY in Q2 2026. Q3 2026 is the first data point within the promised inflection window. Binary test inbound.
- **Co-CEO quality divergence.** Söderström's Q&A is substantively more candid than Norstrom's. Norstrom's pattern of social deflection before engaging questions, and refusal to discuss ARPU timing or pricing elasticity, creates a credibility asymmetry that analysts are beginning to probe.
- **Emerging markets pivot introduced without prior framing.** The decision to deliberately increase friction in emerging markets free tiers — producing intentional near-term MAU compression — appeared simultaneously with the quarter it manifested (Q2 2026), not in prior guidance. Q3 subscriber guidance of +5M (vs. Q2's +7M actual) is the first measurable consequence.
- **Perishable goal pattern.** "Year of accelerated execution" → "year of raising ambition" without structured accounting of what was delivered. Super Fan tier, a la carte transactions, and the 1 billion subscriber ambition all carry no intermediate milestones or timelines.
- **AI content risk underexamined.** The "13 years and nobody has beaten our personalization" argument, deployed at Investor Day, relies on pre-generative-AI historical precedent. The structural question — whether LLM-enabled competitors can replicate the taste-data advantage via API partnerships — is acknowledged but not stress-tested.

## Verify Next Quarter

The following commitments from Q2 2026 should be checked when Q3 2026 drops (expected late October 2026):

1. **Advertising inflection — binary test.** Management has committed to "double-digit" ad revenue growth in H2 2026 across six consecutive communications. Q3 2026 is the first quarter within that window. Report the actual YoY ad-supported revenue growth rate and compare directly to the commitment. If it remains in the 3–7% range, challenge management to specify what specifically separates their H2 2026 expectation from their prior-period misses.

2. **Emerging markets MAU/subscriber trade-off.** Q3 MAU guided at 788M (vs. 780M actual in Q2) and subscribers at 305M (+5M vs. Q2's +7M). Verify whether subscriber growth deceleration is within the range Norstrom characterized as consistent with the conversion strategy, or whether both MAU and subscribers disappoint.

3. **OpEx moderation into Q4.** Management committed explicitly that the ~EUR 200M incremental OpEx would moderate in Q4 2026 as marketing concentration eases. Track whether Q3 OpEx guidance and Q4 preliminary commentary confirm this path.

4. **Reserved ticketing scale.** ~100,000 tickets at U.S.-only launch. Track whether the Live Nation allocation upsizes, whether international expansion is announced, and whether any conversion or ARPU impact is disclosed.

5. **Audiobooks+ ARR trajectory.** Crossed $100M ARR "this July" (Investor Day). Q3 disclosure should reveal whether it is compounding or plateauing — key read on power-law monetization thesis viability.

6. **"Meaningful FCF growth" quantification.** Now in its third consecutive quarter without a range. If Q3 does not provide a number, this becomes a documented declining precision signal from an otherwise exemplary financial communicator.

7. **Song DNA user trajectory.** "52 million users in 4 weeks" was cited in Q1 2026. A Q3 update would indicate whether launch-week virality translated to sustained engagement or followed the typical viral decay curve.
