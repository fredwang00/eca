**Structural constant:** In every quarter, Dimension 1 (Capital Stewardship) is the score's ceiling and Dimension 2 (Strategic Clarity) is its floor. CFO financial disclosure has consistently outperformed CEO strategic disclosure by approximately one full grade across the entire dataset.

## FOG Patterns

**Recurring phrases across quarters (cited by name):**

1. **"We could not be more excited"** — appears in Q3 FY2023, Q1 FY2024, Q2 FY2024, Q3 FY2024, Q1 FY2025, Q2 FY2025, Q3 FY2026. Functions as a universal deflection closer. Never attached to a specific metric.

2. **"Revolutionary" / "most advanced personal electronics device ever"** — applied to Vision Pro in Q3 FY2023, Q1 FY2024. Applied to Apple Intelligence features in Q3 FY2024 onward. Self-referential superlative with no competitive benchmark.

3. **"We will look at a range of options"** — debuted in Q2 FY2026 in response to memory cost pricing questions; repeated verbatim in Q3 FY2026. Explicitly called out in analyses as a conversation-stopper.

4. **"Our MO has always been to do work, then talk about work"** — Cook's AI deflection in Q1 FY2024, used nearly identically across Q4 FY2023 and Q2 FY2024. Pre-scripted avoidance, not genuine candor.

5. **"Deeply personal and private"** — Apple's AI positioning phrase. Appears in Q3 FY2024, Q1 FY2025, Q2 FY2025, Q1 FY2026, Q3 FY2026. Repeated verbatim without evolution in meaning or supporting metrics.

6. **"It feels like magic"** — applied to Apple Watch gesture (Q4 FY2023) and Vision Pro (Q4 FY2023). Emotional substitution for technical explanation.

7. **"Best work is yet to come"** / **"Our roadmap is incredible"** — Cook closing boilerplate, present in Q4 FY2023, Q3 FY2024, Q1 FY2025, Q4 FY2025, Q1 FY2026, Q3 FY2026.

**Trajectory assessment:** Linguistic precision has **improved marginally but inconsistently.** Parekh (replacing Maestri from Q1 FY2025 onward) maintains a comparable standard of numerical precision. Cook's FOG load in prepared remarks has been stable throughout the dataset — it is not improving. The most notable improvement is in Q2–Q3 FY2026: (1) Cook labeled his own deflection explicitly ("I'm dodging the question intentionally"), and (2) the tariff decomposition was volunteered without prompting. These are discrete positive datapoints against an otherwise consistent FOG baseline. The Q&A zone remains the weakest point: roughly half of forward-looking strategic questions receive non-substantive responses across the full dataset.

## Flags & Risks

**Data quality flags:**
- **Q2 FY2026 revenue and OCF discrepancy:** Stated revenue $111.2B vs. structured data $109,417M (~$1.8B gap); stated OCF $28.7B vs. structured data $34,369M (~$5.7B gap). Not reconciled in the call. Warrants 10-Q cross-reference.
- **Total equity jump** from $65,830M to $107,520M over four quarters is unexplained in call narratives. No management commentary attributes this to a specific event.
- **CapEx narrative vs. data divergence:** Management describes "substantially increasing" AI infrastructure investment; structured CapEx data shows a declining trend from -$3,462M (Q2 FY2025) to -$1,971M (Q1 FY2026). Resolution is that Apple routes AI infrastructure costs through partner balance sheets (acknowledged by Cook), but this is an asymmetric information position — investors cannot size Apple's true AI capital commitment from public filings alone.

**Communication-level risks (aggregated across quarters):**
- **Personalized Siri as perishable goal:** Committed "this year" in Q3 FY2025, then "next year" in Q4 FY2025 and Q1 FY2026, then "coming this year" again in Q3 FY2026. Feature definition, milestone, and recovery explanation have never been provided. This is the clearest recurring accountability gap in the dataset.
- **Google TAC / default search exposure:** Estimated at $18–20B annually by analysts; Apple has not confirmed the figure. The DOJ antitrust case was active from Q4 FY2023 onward. Cook's response throughout: subject change or explicit refusal. The September quarter guidance in Q3 FY2025 explicitly assumed "the current revenue share agreement with Google continues" — an undisclosed, material figure embedded in a guidance condition.
- **Safari search decline evasion (Q3 FY2025):** A direct question about whether Safari search activity is declining received two sentences and zero data. In context of the Google TAC relationship being in litigation, this is the most financially material non-answer in the dataset.
- **$500B / $600B U.S. investment:** Round number, shifting total across quarters, no CapEx/OpEx breakdown after direct questioning. Treated by analyses as a political alignment gesture rather than capital allocation commitment.
- **China structural response absent:** Cook acknowledges China as "the most competitive market in the world" across every quarter but provides no operational response to Huawei's re-emergence. Confidence is asserted without a described plan.
- **Services margin compression unexplained (Q3 FY2024):** Services gross margin declined 60bps sequentially with no causal explanation from Parekh. Services acceleration to 15% in Q4 FY2025 was described as "broad-based" with "nothing abnormal" — the absence of explanation for both deceleration and acceleration limits investor modeling.
- **Incoming CEO communication void:** John Ternus's contribution to Q2 FY2026 and Q3 FY2026 was entirely aspirational — no strategic priorities, no risk acknowledgments, no falsifiable commitments. The transition quality is not yet evaluable from disclosed content.
